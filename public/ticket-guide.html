Ticket Workflow Guide

How to claim, handle, and resolve tickets — and what each state means.

Where tickets come from
🤖
AI Chatbot
A guest starts a conversation with the chatbot on the website. When they leave an email address or the chatbot can't fully help, the conversation becomes a ticket automatically.

The first message in the ticket shows the full chatbot transcript so you have full context before you reply.
📋
Contact Form
A guest fills out the contact form on the website or an incoming email is received. A ticket is created instantly with their name, email, phone number, and message.

The source badge on each ticket row shows which channel the inquiry came through.
How you reply — same in both cases
1
Open the ticket — click the row to expand the message thread.
2
Type your reply in the reply box at the bottom of the thread — regardless of whether the ticket came from the chatbot or the contact form.
3
Hit Send — your reply is delivered automatically. Where it goes depends on the source (see below).
📋
Contact Form — reply goes as an email. The guest's reply comes back into the same ticket thread automatically.
🤖
Chatbot — under 10 min old — reply appears directly in the guest's open chatbot window. They see it in real time.
⚠️
Chatbot — over 10 min old — the guest has likely left the chatbot window. Instead of replying in the ticket, reach out directly via WhatsApp. Nobody waits 10+ minutes in a chat window.
Tickets created by bookings
Some tickets are not messages from guests. The booking system opens them by itself when a person has to decide something. They carry the badge Internal and are closed once the decision is made. In the sandbox they are test tickets and only admins see them.
⏱
Assign time slots
When: a course or a Scuba Skills Update is booked and some days have no time yet ("to be set").

What you do: expand the ticket, choose AM, PM or full day and the start time for each open day, save. The student gets a mail with the plan. The ticket closes when no open day is left.
✅
Attendance check
When: after a dive day, for every booking without a completed check-in.

What you do: press Was there (booking completed, thank-you mail goes out), Trip cancelled (we cancelled the day, the booking stays) or No-show (seats released, no refund). The system never guesses, so a forgotten check-in cannot create a no-show.
🛟
DAN correction
When: a dive day that was already submitted to DAN is moved or cancelled.

What you do: correct or withdraw the entry in the DAN portal by hand (DAN has no API), then resolve the ticket.
💡
Time slot tickets have a button that opens the full booking (payments, sessions, change log).

A red number on a ticket row means the customer sent new inbound messages that you haven't read yet — specifically, messages received after your last outbound reply.

To clear the badge: open the ticket and scroll down to the last message. Once it's in view, the badge disappears automatically — no button needed.

No badge = no new messages since your last reply. If you just sent a reply, the badge won't appear until the customer writes back.

What to do
🟠 Open — nobody claimed
Click Claim to put your name on it. Removes it from the "All unclaimed" view.
⚡ Claimed — by you
Unclaim puts it back into the pool — e.g. before your shift ends. Any colleague can then take it.
⚡ Claimed — by colleague
Click Take Over if a colleague is off and the ticket needs handling now. Name switches to you.
When to use each action
🟠 Claim this ticket when…
You replied and are waiting for the guest
Travel dates, group size, certification level still missing
Payment or deposit outstanding
Booking started but not yet complete
You're checking availability
Waiting on an internal confirmation before replying
✅ Mark as Resolved when…
Question answered
No further action needed — no reply expected
Booking confirmed & paid
Booking is complete in the system
Complaint handled
Solution communicated to the guest
Cancelled or no response
After follow-up with no reply
The rule: Resolved means we've done our part. No guest confirmation needed — what counts is whether anything is still outstanding on our end.
Ownership: Before you go off shift, either Resolve or Unclaim any open ticket you hold. Don't leave it claimed with no one watching.
